Welcome To Zest Heaven

A Warm, Cozy Haven For Great Bites & Fresh Brews

Zest Heaven is a casual and welcoming café/food destination offering quick bites, coffee, shakes, juices, small plates and a comfortable dining atmosphere designed for students, families, and food lovers alike.

Located right in front of Law College at Bundelkhand University, Jhansi, we bring you high-quality taste and serene café vibes at truly affordable prices. Whether you're hanging out with friends after lectures or having a quiet meal solo, Zest Heaven is your perfect spot.
